How records are flagged
Waste % = Waste ÷ Available — red when over 30%
Leftover % = Leftover ÷ Available — red when over 40%
Served % = Total Served ÷ Available — flagged when under 30%
Balance — flagged when Served + Unservable + Leftover ≠ Available

Export reports

Scroll down and generate whichever report you need. Each one pulls from the same live Firebase records. Individual Meal Count Sheets (Attachment 18) are exported per record from the print/export buttons in Site Lookup → All sites.

Per-Site Monthly Count Report

A workbook for one site across a selected month range, in the state report template: Daily Meal Details (one row per weekday Mon–Sat with a totals row — open days show counts, unsubmitted days show blank, and days the site marked closed on its Availability calendar show CLOSED), Monthly Averages and Monthly Totals (one row per month), and a Site Information sheet. Pick a site and the first & last month to include.

Weekly Waste & Leftover Report

A week-by-week workbook (Mon–Sat) showing waste and leftover for every site: a Summary sheet with each site's totals and waste/leftover percentage of available, plus Waste by Day and Leftover by Day matrices. Pick any date inside the week you want — the report snaps back to that week's Monday. Use it to spot sites with recurring waste or over-ordering.

Site Closed-Dates Report

A monthly workbook listing every site's closed dates — the weekdays each site unchecked on its Availability calendar — with how many service days stayed open. A Closed Summary sheet gives one row per site (closed count, open count, and the closed-date list), and a Closed Dates sheet gives one row per closed date. Pulls live from each site's calendar; use it to reconcile missing submissions against planned closures.

Site Audit Report

A detailed audit workbook for a single site across a date range, matching the CACFP audit format: daily meal details, monthly averages, monthly totals, and a site information sheet. Use this when a monitor or state reviewer requests one site's full history.

Monthly Audit Report

A month-wide workbook across all sites (or one site): the Sponsor Monthly Meal Count Summary by site, a review-flags sheet listing every anomaly, and a raw daily-detail sheet. Use this for the monthly claim review and the compliance department's summary.

Initial Estimated Reimbursement

A single-sheet workbook matching the compliance department's format: per-site Received / Available / Attendance / 1st & 2nd meals, with Real 2nds capped at MIN(2nd, 1st×2%), a combined total, and an estimated reimbursement at the $4.90 multiplier. This is the initial estimate handed off for the monthly claim.

All Records (CSV)

Every record across every site as a flat CSV — one row per meal record with all fields. Use this for spreadsheets, pivot tables, or importing into another system.
